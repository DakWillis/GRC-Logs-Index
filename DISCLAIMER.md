# Disclaimer

Every scenario in this repository is sanitized and generalized. No real organization names, staff names, email addresses, asset serial numbers, IP addresses, hostnames, internal system URLs, vendor contracts, or pricing appear anywhere in this repository.

Environments are described at a level of detail sufficient to make the control scenario meaningful and insufficient to identify any specific organization. Where a scenario resembles a real environment, the resemblance is to a condition that is common across many organizations rather than to a particular one.

This material is not legal, audit, or compliance advice. Framework mappings represent one practitioner's reading of the applicable controls and are provided for learning and discussion. Validate all control requirements against the current published standard and with your own security, audit, or compliance function before implementing anything here in a live environment.

Framework references are to ISO/IEC 27001:2022, SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria, NIST CSF 2.0, and CIS Controls v8.1. Control numbering changes between framework versions — confirm you are working from the version this repository references before relying on a mapping.
